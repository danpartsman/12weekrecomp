Training creates demand. Food provides building material and fuel. Recovery lets adaptation happen. Consistency gives the process time.

Move.

Use your muscles in ways you enjoy and can repeat.

Fuel.

Eat enough protein and place energy and carbohydrate intelligently around demand.

Recover.

Respect sleep, soreness and what your body actually did.

Adapt.

Watch trends and change the focus as body composition and performance evolve.

It does not happen in a day.

Over a number of weeks, consistent training and nutrition can produce changes you see, feel or measure. The target is roughly 6 good days out of 7 while keeping the strategy realistic enough to live with.
